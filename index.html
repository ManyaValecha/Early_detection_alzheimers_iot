⚙️ Methodology

Research Methodology

Rigorous zero-leakage participant splits, SHAP explainability, and clinically validated evaluation protocols.

🛡️

Zero Participant Leakage

GroupShuffleSplit along patient_id ensures Train ∩ Val ∩ Test = ∅. All tuning on validation; final metrics on a single held-out test evaluation.

🔬

5 Candidate Algorithms

Model 1 evaluates Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and a PyTorch deep neural network. Best is selected on validation AUC.

📉

Bootstrap 95% CIs

1,000 bootstrap resamples provide confidence intervals for all test-set AUC figures, ensuring statistical robustness of results.

🌟

SHAP Explainability

TreeExplainer and LinearExplainer generate global beeswarm and local waterfall charts. Top contributors: Cognitive Composite, MMSE, CDR, APOE-ε4.

📡

TIHM Cohort (Nature 2023)

56 participants, 2,800 participant-days from Palermo et al. IoT telemetry. Rolling z-score baseline deviation features for event prediction.

🧮

Geometric Mean Combination

Combined AUC = √(AUC₁ × AUC₂) = √(0.99 × 0.843) = 0.9136. Balances both models without over-weighting either stream.
